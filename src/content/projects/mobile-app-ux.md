## Challenge

A seed-stage health startup needed to ship a consumer-facing app to validate their core hypothesis: that a simplified, non-judgmental daily check-in habit could improve users' subjective wellbeing over 30 days. They had 14 weeks and a two-person engineering team.

## Actions

I joined as mobile lead and immediately ran a three-day discovery sprint with the founder to prioritize scope: one core loop (daily check-in → insight → streak), onboarding, and push notifications. I designed the UX in Figma alongside building in React Native with Expo, which let a single codebase target iOS and Android simultaneously.

The most significant investment was in the onboarding flow. Research showed that health apps live or die by day-3 retention. I ran five guerrilla usability tests in the first two weeks to iterate on the onboarding narrative and personalization moment, reducing the onboarding drop-off from 54% to 18% before launch.

I used Supabase for the backend (auth, database, edge functions), which meant no dedicated backend engineer was needed, keeping the team at two.

## Results

The app launched to the App Store in week 14, on schedule. It received a 4.7-star rating from 312 reviews in the first 30 days. Day-7 retention hit 68% — nearly 3× the category average — which the founder used to close a €1.2M seed round two months after launch.
