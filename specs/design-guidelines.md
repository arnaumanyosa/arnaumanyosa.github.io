# Design Guidelines

## Concept
Minimalistic design focused on typography and whitespace usage.

## Color Palette
- Primary Body Text: #323232
- Title/Heading Text: #1a1a1a
- Subtitle Text: #4d4d4d
- Interactive Elements (Hover): #666666
- Accent/Border: #808080

## Typography
- Titles and Headings: Poppins
- Body Text and Content: DM Sans

## Spacing, Borders, and Elevation
Following the frontend-design skill guidelines for consistent spacing, border radius, and elevation effects.
